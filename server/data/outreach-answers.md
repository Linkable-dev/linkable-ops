# Answers to what people ask when we write to them

Verified on 22 Nov 2026 against the questions and answers published on linkable.link (creators, Pitch, pricing and home pages) and against how the app pays creators. Used only by the reply agent (server/lib/outreach-answers.js), next to the verified facts.

## What creators ask

- Is it free? Yes. Creating a profile, applying to campaigns and pitching brands is always free for creators. Pitch is free for 10 pitches a month, follow ups included.
- Does Linkable take a cut of what a brand pays me? No. A creator receives the whole commission and the whole fixed fee they agreed. Linkable's fees are paid by the brand, on top.
- Do I need a big following? No. Brands look for creators who fit their audience, not just the biggest numbers, and many campaigns are open to smaller creators.
- How do I get paid? Commission is tracked through the creator's own link and paid out to them. Fixed fees are agreed with each brand before the creator starts.
- Which platforms? Instagram and TikTok. The creator connects their accounts and their audience insights are added to their profile.
- Which brands can I pitch? Any brand they want to work with, including brands that are not on Linkable yet. Linkable also suggests brands that fit their niche and audience.
- Who receives my pitch? Linkable finds the person who runs creator partnerships at the brand and sends the pitch to them, not to a generic inbox.
- Can I edit the pitch before it is sent? Yes. Everything drafted for them is theirs to change, from the idea to what they will create and what they ask for.
- Who owns the content I make? The creator does. Usage rights are agreed per campaign, so they always know how a brand can use their content.
- Which countries? Brands on Linkable work with creators in many countries, and each campaign shows where it ships. A creator can pitch a brand in any country.
- Can I cancel Pitch Pro? Yes, any time. They keep Pro until the end of the billing period, then move back to the free plan.

## What brands ask

- Do I need a Shopify store? No. Linkable works without Shopify. Connecting it adds product sync, automatic discounts and sales attribution.
- What can I offer creators? Free product, a fixed fee, commission on sales, or any mix of the three, set per campaign.
- How do I find the right creators? Creators in the network apply to the campaign, matching suggests the best fits with the reasons, and the brand can invite creators directly. The brand always chooses who it works with.
- What is the 2.5% fee? It applies only to sales tracked through creator links. Everything else is covered by the plan.
- What counts towards the monthly creator limit? Each new creator the brand starts working with in a month counts once. Applications and invitations are unlimited on every plan.
- Which currency? Prices are shown in US dollars. UK brands pay in British pounds, brands in Europe pay in euros, and everyone else pays in US dollars.
- Who owns the content? Usage rights are agreed in each campaign and saved with every piece of content in the brand's library.
- Can your team run it for us? Yes. The managed service plans the campaigns, finds the creators and manages every collaboration. What it costs depends on the campaigns, so that is a conversation with the team: hand over.
- Can I change plan or cancel? Yes, any time.

## Where we got their address

- A creator: from their public Instagram profile, where they list an email for work enquiries.
- A brand: we look for a contact address for the shop, usually the one on its own website. Say it that way ("usually"): for this one shop you do not know which page it was on, so do not state that it was on their website.
- Say this plainly if they ask, and that they only need to say so and we will not write again. Asking where we got the address is a fair question, not a complaint.
- A request to see, correct or delete what we hold about them, or any mention of the law, a regulator or a complaint, is not answered here: hand over.
