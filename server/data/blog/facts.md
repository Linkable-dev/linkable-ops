# Verified facts about Linkable (source: linkable.link, Nov 2026)

Only these facts may be stated as facts. Anything else about Linkable must be left out.

## What Linkable is
- A creator-collaboration platform for ecommerce brands. Brands run gifted, paid and affiliate creator campaigns, receive applications from creators, discover and invite creators, manage collaborations, get content, and track results in one place.
- Campaign types: gifted product (free product), paid collaboration (a fixed fee), affiliate commission (creators earn a percentage on tracked sales), or a mix.
- The platform matches creators to campaigns with AI-powered matching and lets brands review applications, or discover and invite creators who match the brand.
- Shopify integration: product catalogue and orders sync from Shopify; gifted product orders can be created automatically on approval; affiliate sales are tracked through unique creator links and discount codes, with commission payouts handled through the platform.
- Reporting shows content delivered, audiences reached, engagement generated and tracked sales per campaign and per creator.
- Creators start applying to a new campaign within 24 hours (site claim: "<24h first applications").
- Linkable also offers a managed service where the Linkable team plans campaigns, sources creators and manages collaborations for the brand ("Prefer a hands-off approach?" on the homepage; contact via /contact).

## Pricing for brands (pricing page, Nov 2026, prices in US dollars)
- Free: $0, for brands working with their own creators. Unlimited private campaigns, the brand's own application link to share, no card needed.
- Starter: $99 per month (or $990 a year, saving 17%). Up to 10 new creators a month and up to 2 public campaigns at once.
- Growth (most popular): $199 per month (or $1,990 a year). Up to 50 new creators a month and up to 10 public campaigns at once.
- Scale: $499 per month (or $4,990 a year). Up to 100 new creators a month and up to 20 public campaigns at once, advanced campaign and creator insights, priority support and onboarding.
- Every plan, Free included, adds 2.5% of creator driven sales.
- Paid plans start with a free trial of 30 days. Change plan or cancel any time.
- Every paid plan includes the full platform: any collaboration type, unlimited applications and invitations, AI creator matching and campaign setup, messaging and briefs, shipping and tracking, a content library with usage rights tracked, affiliate tracking and payouts. Private campaigns are unlimited on every plan.
- Public campaigns are the ones creators on Linkable can find and apply to. Private campaigns are for the brand's own creators.
- Brands sign up at app.linkable.link. Connecting a Shopify store is optional; with it, products and orders sync. Linkable is also listed on the Shopify App Store (apps.shopify.com/linkable-1).

## For creators (creators page and Pitch page, Nov 2026)
- Joining Linkable and applying to brand campaigns is free for creators. Creators apply to brand campaigns, with the reward shown before they apply: free product, a fixed fee, affiliate commission on tracked sales, or a mix.
- A creator connects Instagram or TikTok and their audience insights fill in on their profile.
- Products are shipped to the creator, content is reviewed and approved in one place, and payments are tracked in one place.
- Pitch: a creator can pitch any brand, not only brands on Linkable. Linkable writes the pitch from the creator's profile, the creator can edit it before it is sent, and can attach their own proposal or media kit.
- Linkable suggests brands that fit the creator's niche and audience, and puts first the ones actively looking for creators.
- Linkable looks for the person who runs creator partnerships at the brand and sends the pitch to them, and sends follow ups for the creator.
- An accepted pitch becomes a collaboration on Linkable.
- Pitch is free for 10 pitches a month, follow ups included, with no card needed.
- Pitch Pro: $19 per month (or $190 a year, saving 17%). Unlimited pitches to brands on Linkable, 100 pitches a month to any other brand, and Auto Pitch, which pitches 25 brands for the creator every week.
- Brands look for creators whose audience fits their products, not only large followings. Pitch works for creators of any size.
