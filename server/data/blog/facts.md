Only these facts may be stated as facts. Anything else about Linkable must be left out.

## What Linkable is
- A creator-collaboration platform for ecommerce brands, built for Shopify. Brands run gifted, paid and affiliate creator campaigns, receive applications from creators, discover and invite creators, manage collaborations, get content, and track results in one place.
- Campaign types: gifted product (free product), paid collaboration (a fixed fee), affiliate commission (creators earn a percentage on tracked sales), or a mix.
- The platform matches creators to campaigns with AI-powered matching and lets brands review applications, or discover and invite creators who match the brand.
- Shopify integration: product catalogue and orders sync from Shopify; gifted product orders can be created automatically on approval; affiliate sales are tracked through unique creator links and discount codes, with commission payouts handled through the platform.
- Reporting shows content delivered, audiences reached, engagement generated and tracked sales per campaign and per creator.
- Creators start applying to a new campaign within 24 hours (site claim: "<24h first applications").
- Linkable also offers a managed service where the Linkable team plans campaigns, sources creators and manages collaborations for the brand ("Prefer a hands-off approach?" on the homepage; contact via /contact).

## Pricing (pricing page, Sep 2026)
- Starter: $99 per month (or $990 per year, saving 17%) plus 2.9% of tracked sales. 10 creator collaborations per month, up to 2 creator campaigns, unlimited applications and invitations, AI campaign setup and creator matching, campaign and creator insights, affiliate tracking and payouts, Shopify product and order sync, dedicated support and onboarding.
- Growth (most popular): $199 per month (or $1,990 per year) plus 2.9% of tracked sales. 50 creator collaborations per month, up to 10 campaigns, otherwise the Starter feature set.
- Scale: $299 per month (or $2,990 per year) plus 2.9% of tracked sales. 100 creator collaborations per month, up to 20 campaigns, advanced campaign and creator insights, priority support and onboarding.
- 30-day money-back guarantee. Cancel anytime.
- Linkable is installed from the Shopify App Store (apps.shopify.com/linkable-1).

## For creators (creators page)
